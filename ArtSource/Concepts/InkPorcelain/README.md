## Design decisions

- Knight: recognizable horse profile, broad cobalt mane and neck panel, angular crest.
- Bishop: a deep diagonal slot and tall pointed head, with a teal inset below it.
- Rook: broad square battlements with dark separations and terracotta recesses.
- Shared set: warm ivory ceramic, charcoal matte foot, narrow gold trim, sparse ink accents.
- Keep the distinctive top shapes visible from the gameplay camera; prioritize broad color areas over tiny ornament.
